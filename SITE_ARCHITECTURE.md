# LRMC Site Architecture

## Product purpose

Create an unofficial, mission-led digital experience for Landstuhl Regional Medical Center that serves three connected needs:

1. Explain who LRMC is, why it exists, and how it supports military medicine.
2. Recruit prospective team members and route them to current official opportunities.
3. Guide people who have received a Tentative Job Offer from TJO through arrival and their first 90 days.

LRMC is the organizing center of the experience. Germany is the surrounding relocation and community context.

## Primary audiences

- Prospective employees exploring LRMC and overseas service
- Candidates who have received a Tentative Job Offer
- Incoming military, civilian, and family members preparing to relocate
- Current team members looking for orientation or practical resources
- Patients, families, partners, and community members learning about LRMC

## Homepage routing

The homepage establishes LRMC's mission and then routes visitors by intent:

1. Learn about LRMC
2. Explore careers
3. I received a TJO
4. Prepare for arrival
5. Find resources

The hero should lead with military medicine and human purpose, not relocation logistics. The TJO pathway remains a highly visible primary action.

## Institutional page model

### Who We Are

Purpose: Introduce LRMC's distinctive role without reading like a generic hospital profile.

Sections:

- The only forward-stationed U.S. medical center supporting U.S. and coalition forces
- A jointly staffed medical center serving Europe, the Middle East, and Africa
- Medical specialties, trauma capability, and beneficiary reach
- The people behind the mission: military, civilian, local-national, and partner teams
- Route to Mission, History, Leadership, Careers, and Joining LRMC

### Why We Exist

Purpose: Connect patient care to operational readiness and the Joint Warfighter.

Narrative pillars:

- Ready Medical Force
- Medically Ready Force
- Safe, high-quality, compassionate care
- Strategic evacuation and treatment capability
- Resilient families and strong partnerships

### Mission, Vision, and Values

Purpose: Present current official language clearly and distinguish sourced statements from editorial interpretation.

Official mission and vision copy must remain attributable and easy to update. Supporting editorial pillars can explain readiness, teamwork, selfless service, quality, safety, and compassion in plain language.

### History and Legacy

Purpose: Show how LRMC's role developed and why its legacy matters now.

Structure:

- Founding and early mission
- Evolution of overseas military medicine
- Role in aeromedical evacuation and contingency response
- Post-2001 wounded-warrior care
- Seventy years of selfless service
- The next chapter of military medicine in Europe

Exact dates, facility names, milestones, and statistics require official-source verification before publication.

## Leadership page model

- Current commander and command sergeant major
- Short verified biographies with official profile links
- Commander's philosophy presented as a dedicated editorial statement
- Command priorities translated into practical expectations for the LRMC team
- Leadership data must be treated as time-sensitive and verified before every release

The commander's philosophy must not be invented from general mission language. Publish it only from user-provided or official command-team material.

## Careers page model

### Recruitment story

- Practice military medicine where readiness and care intersect
- Serve a joint and international community
- Develop professionally in a high-consequence medical environment
- Live and work in Germany
- Join as military, federal civilian, local national, contractor, trainee, or partner where applicable

### Vacancies

Preferred progression:

1. Launch with authoritative, filtered USAJOBS links and clear search guidance.
2. Add a live vacancy feed only when a stable official API/query strategy and attribution are verified.
3. Always provide a direct USAJOBS fallback if the feed is unavailable.

Vacancy cards should show title, organization, location, closing date, appointment type, and an official application link. The site must never imply that applying through this unofficial site submits an application.

## TJO landing page model

Hero: "You received a TJO. Start here."

The page should reduce uncertainty immediately and explain that requirements vary by employment category and authorizing office.

## Visual direction

Retain the current interaction quality, cards, responsive behavior, and journey system. Replace the pastoral companion aesthetic with a mission-led visual language:

- Deep navy, surgical blue, warm white, restrained field olive, and limited readiness-red accents
- Strong condensed display typography paired with highly readable body type
- Authentic imagery of clinical teams, readiness, patient care, evacuation, partnership, and the LRMC campus
- Subtle grids, coordinates, topographic lines, medical traces, and operational data motifs
- Precise motion and staged reveals rather than decorative animation
- Approximately 60% mission/readiness and 40% care/community

## Media locations

- Still photography and images: `public/pictures/`
- Video, audio, animation, and other media: `public/media/`

## Content governance

- The experience is an unofficial public edition and must say so clearly.
- Official requirements, eligibility, deadlines, benefits, leadership, vacancies, mission statements, and statistics require authoritative links and periodic verification.
- Do not imply affiliation with LRMC, DHA, the Department of Defense, or another government organization.
- Do not expose personal, medical, applicant, or employment data.
- Do not reproduce protected marks or imagery without appropriate authorization.
